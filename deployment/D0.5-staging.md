The gameplay spec §11 requires an always-on host without idle sleep for the deployed game. That motivated the earlier paid-instance advice, but paid compute is not technically required for these initial smoke tests. Free can exercise startup, HTTPS/WebSockets, multiplayer, and reconnect while the service is awake. This user-authorized staging choice does not establish production hosting suitability.

Render Free spins down after 15 minutes without inbound HTTP or WebSocket messages; waking it takes about one minute. Render can also restart Free services at any time, and Free has usage limits and no service shell access. Sleep/restart loses this application's in-memory rooms: reconnect after sleep cannot restore the old match. Test same-process reconnect while awake, and record cold-start or process-replacement behavior separately. Do not disguise the sleep limitation with an artificial keepalive service. D0.5 remains incomplete until real startup, assets/HTTPS/WebSockets, multiplayer, reconnect, and counterpart review are verified and documented.
