## 6. Rulings made during implementation

- **Prop rotation:** dressing `rotationY` may be any quarter turn, not only 0 or π/2. Benches could not otherwise face the plaza.
- **Test loader:** Nature props in the town test load as boxes at their declared size. A unit box scales to a 6.5-wide cube and is not representative. Declared footprints are now the measured model bounds, rounded up to 0.05.
- **Fountain clearance:** the fountain is checked as a circle, not as a bounding box. The box corner falsely enters a crowd disk.
- **Lamps near doors:** lamps stay out of each door's zone, door x ±2.5 and façade to +5. The plan's ±1.5 missed the lamp that visibly hid the town hall door. That lamp was dropped.
- **Office walls and door:** walls use `wood` and the door uses `trim`. Paper red was nearly invisible on brick, and a wood door vanished on wood walls.
- **Dropped flower box:** the town hall flower box is gone. Its centred door and crowd disk leave no room for one.
