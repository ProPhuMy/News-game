# C3 — Complete architecture: implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build every required recognizable building and the town's props on the unchanged map, keep all routes and crowd spaces clear, and deliver it through the existing `createTownBuildings(layout, ctx)` entry point with inspected screenshots.

**Architecture:** Code-built pieces (approved at D1) follow the C2 pattern: a factory takes an `ArchitectureContext` and returns a `PlacedPiece`. A new `architecture/town.ts` assembles the context-path town: each building body sits in the block lot behind its node, and a fixed dressing table places props. `createTownBuildings(layout, ctx)` delegates to it, so Codex's D2 wiring stays one call. The no-context primitive path is unchanged until D2 switches over. A new harness scene renders the assembled town through `npm run capture -- --target architecture --view town|<close-up>`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript (strict), Three.js 0.186, Vitest, the shared `playwright-core` capture (C0). No new dependencies, no new downloaded assets.

## User decisions (2026-10-19, this session)

- **Placement:** each building body sits in the city block lot just north (−Z, away from the camera) of its node. Its front faces +Z toward the street, with the door nearest the node. The crowd stands on the street in front. Nothing straddles a road. Nodes, routes and logical anchors do not move.
- **Crowd clearance:** keep a radius of **5** clear around the nine gathering nodes (five targets, four offices). Keep **3.5** clear around every other node (flee spots, homes). Road strips stay fully clear everywhere.
- **Paperboys:** Codex places them in D2 as static Kenney Mini figures. C3 only exports checked corner spots.

## Lot geometry (decided here, checked by tests)

Offsets from the node, x east, z south (+Z toward the camera). A lot on side `east` spans x `[4, 4 + w]`; `west` spans `[−4 − w, −4]`. Every lot spans z `[−3 − d, −3]`. So the corner nearest the node is (4, 3), exactly 5 from it.

| Piece | Node | Side | Footprint w × d |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bakery (C2, unchanged) | `LOCATION_NODES.bakery` | east | 6 × 5 |
| Bank | `bank` | west | 6 × 5 |
| Town hall | `town_hall` | west | 6 × 5 (tower above) |
| Clinic | `clinic` | east | 6 × 5 |
| Office seat 0, 1, 2, 3 | `OFFICE_NODES[i]` | east, west, east, west | 5 × 4.4 |
| Fountain square | `fountain_square` | root on the node | plaza paving (flat) plus a fountain basin radius ≤ 3.3 centered at offset (6, −6) |

Bottom offices are 4.4 deep because a target node sits on the block's opposite corner (5 needed). Hand-checked: each lot clears its own node at 5, its block's other corners at 3.5 or 5, and road strips at 2.0. The tests below are the authority.

## Review Focus

1. **A prop or wall lands in a road or a crowd disk** after someone nudges the dressing table. The town clearance test (Task 5) checks every mesh below `HEADROOM` and every Nature footprint, so this fails loudly.
2. **Office banner recolor shares a cached kit material.** `syncOffices` must swap to `surface(newHex)`, never mutate a kit material's color, or every brick-red surface would change. Pinned in Task 3.
3. **Highlight then recolor (or the reverse)** keeps both states: a highlighted office that changes paper color stays highlighted in the new color. Pinned in Task 3.
4. **A Nature model fails to load anywhere in the town.** The stand-in renders, `ready` lists every `degraded` entry, and the capture fails. Pinned in Task 5.
5. **Dispose twice, or dispose before loads finish.** No throw. No piece re-attaches. Sign textures are freed once. Pinned in Tasks 1 and 5.
